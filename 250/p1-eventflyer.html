Project: Event Flyer

Design a flyer to promote and celebrate an imaginary event. Come up with a detailed concept for an event tailored to your specific interests and/or hobbies. Your process will include making a moodboard, sketching, creating raster-based images in Photoshop, and exploring different compositions to integrate text and image to convey a clear message or theme.

Examples

Learning Goals:

  • Create raster-based images in Photoshop
  • Communicate information to an audience
  • Integrate text and image

Part 1

  1. Imagine an event. It can be a concert, a birthday party, a pop-up shop, etc. You can use this randomizer (by Sasha Portis) or come up with your own ideas. Remember the event must be fictional and original.

    Tip: Be Specific.
    Let’s say you are having a “concert on the moon.” Who is the band, musician or performer? Where can people find this event? For example, “location: on the moon” is not specific enough. Who is the event for?

  2. Brainstorm information about your event
    • the title — Is it short and catchy? Is it memorable?
    • the location — Be specific! You can use real places.
    • the time and date

  3. Create a moodboard using Photoshop.
    1. Collect a minimum of 10 images, including color palettes and styles for reference. 
      Tip: Save or download the images because you may need them for your flyer
    2. Open Photoshop/Photopea. Create a new document, 11 x 8.5 inches (landscape orientation)
    3. Add a textbox in the top-left corner. Include:
      • Your name
      • Type of event
      • Location
      • Date
    4. Add your images to the document.
    5. Rename the file with your full name. Export as a JPG and submit!
      If you don't have Canvas, please send it to Professor L. at cheungl@laccd.edu
      and include "ART 250 moodboard" as the subject title.

Examples

Part 2

  1. Finalize exact information: exact title, exact location, exact time, audience, 1-2 sentence short description (optional).
    1. Fill out your information and list of words on a piece of paper

  2. Sketch your designs on paper. Try many different ideas. Think about composition: How will the text and images be assembled? Think about your audience: Who is this event for? Who do you want to attract?
    1. Sketch sketch sketch. Draw thumbnails in your notebook.

  3. Create a 8½ x 11 INCH document in Photoshop (resolution: 300 ppi).

  4. Pick objects and places that can symbolize your event. Find HIGH RESOLUTION (aka large-sized) photos on the internet and/or use original hi-res photography. Drag and drop to Photoshop:
    1. Minimum of 3 objects as raster images. Remove the background of the images and try to clean up the edges; they are the graphics on your flyer.
      Example: music festival in the desert → guitar + mic + crowd
    2. An additional image as the background.
      Example: music festival in the desert → desert background image

  5. Assemble the flyer; it should have:
    1. Multiple images
    2. Title, date, and location of your event
    3. Option: A short description about the event

  6. Export as JPG and uploadyour flyer to a blank slide to Google Slides
