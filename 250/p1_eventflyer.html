Project: Event Flyer

Design a flyer to promote and celebrate an imaginary event. Come up with a detailed concept for an event tailored to your specific interests and/or hobbies. Your process will include making a moodboard, sketching, creating raster-based images in Photoshop, and exploring different compositions to integrate text and image to convey a clear message or theme.

Examples

Learning Goals:

  • Create raster-based images in Photoshop
  • Communicate information to an audience
  • Integrate text and image

Part 1

  1. Imagine an event. It can be a concert, a birthday party, a pop-up shop, etc. You can use this randomizer (by Sasha Portis) or come up with your own ideas. Remember the event must be fictional and original.

    Tip: Be Specific.
    Let’s say you are having a “concert on the moon.” Who is the band, musician or performer? Where can people find this event? For example, “location: on the moon” is not specific enough. Who is the event for?

  2. Brainstorm information about your event
    • the title — Is it short and catchy? Is it memorable?
    • the location — Be specific! You can use real places.
    • the time and date

  3. Create a moodboard using Photoshop.
    1. Collect a minimum of 10 images, including color palettes and styles for reference. 
      Tip: Save or download the images because you may need them for your flyer
    2. Open Photoshop/Photopea. Create a new document, 11 x 8.5 inches (landscape orientation)
    3. Add a textbox in the top-left corner. Include:
      • Your name
      • Type of event
      • Location
      • Date
    4. Add your images to the document.
    5. Export as a JPG and submit!
