Photo-Painting Assignment

Requirements

  • width: 3000 px
  • height: 2000 px
  • Format: JPG

Instructions

  1. Combine a famous painting and a photograph to create a blended image.
  2. The image must use the following techniques: 
    1. Two or more raster images
    2. Layer masking
    3. Make it look “realistic” by adjusting brightness and contrast.
    4. Blend modes (optional)
  3. Export as JPG. Rename the file with your name.
  4. Submit to Canvas.
    If you don't have Canvas, please send it to Professor L. at cheungl@laccd.edu
    and include "ART 250 photo-painting" as the subject title.

Free-to-use images
